TEAM LOGOS FOR THE TICKER
=========================

Put one logo per team in this folder, named exactly as below (PNG with a transparent
background works best; around 200 pixels tall is plenty). A team without a file just
shows its name, so you can add only the teams you need. Run DEPLOY.cmd (Windows) or
DEPLOY.command (Mac) afterwards.
